## Gaps vs. the current app

Things in Fresha that the Dastaan app does not yet model:

1. **Catalog is far larger** — the app ships 12 mock services; Fresha has 84.
   Nails, facials, waxing, massage, braiding and body clipping are entire
   categories the app has no concept of.
2. **Non-barber roles** — nail artists, nail technicians and receptionists are
   real bookable/staff roles. The app's `Barber` type assumes barbers only.
3. **Packages / multi-service bookings** — Fresha bundles 2–3 services into one
   priced item. The app has no package concept.
4. **Price ranges** — several services are "from AED X" (variable by staff
   seniority or hair length). The app assumes a single fixed price.
5. **Duration ranges** — e.g. "30 min – 45 min". The app assumes one duration.
6. **Memberships, gift cards, loyalty, deals, smart pricing** — all live in
   Fresha, none in the app.
7. **Archived services** — Fresha keeps archived services per category; the app
   has no active/archived distinction beyond a boolean.
